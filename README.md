## Zero-Handshake filter (separate research track)

Research for: **Non-investment-bank financial firms, $1B+ AUM, with a direct USC recruiting relationship, with NO Handshake affiliation at all** (including non-TFA paths).

## What you cannot get from public data

1. **No exhaustive public list** of every $1B+ HF/VC/PE firm with a Hub-only or alumni-only USC pipeline.
2. **Cannot prove negative** — a firm may have zero public Handshake footprint but still have a private Handshake account.
3. **Hub custom resume books** are employer-specific and negotiated via email — firm names are **not published** online except Beach Point (Qualtrics).

## How to actually find every firm (operational playbook)

Email **msbfinancecareerhub@marshall.usc.edu** with exactly:

> "Which buy-side firms (HF, VC, PE, alt credit) currently recruit USC undergrads through Hub resume books, Qualtrics drops, or coffee chats **without** a Handshake posting?"

Also contact **Trojan Investing Society** leadership for:
- Private Investing Day invite list (annual, expanding)
- TIS Buyside program employer contacts

Ask Hub staff to distinguish:
- **Hub-direct employers** (email/Qualtrics/resume book only)
- vs. **Handshake-posted employers** (exclude)
