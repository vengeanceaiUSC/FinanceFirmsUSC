# USC-Connected Financial Firms — 128 entries (100+ requirement met)

**Full dataset:** [`usc_finance_firms_100plus.csv`](usc_finance_firms_100plus.csv)

## Methodology

Each row includes:
- **Firm** with estimated **$1B+ AUM** (or major financial institution scale)
- **USC connection** from Marshall Recruiter Handbook, Trojan Finance Academy, Trojan Investing Society, USC Career Center events, Private Investing Day, or hellofinance
- **Recruiting contact** where publicly identifiable; otherwise flagged for LinkedIn verification
- **Evidence URL** (primary source)

## Limitations

1. **Recruiting head names** are only included where publicly verifiable (LinkedIn, USC event pages, firm posts). For the remaining ~100 firms, the CSV marks `Verify via LinkedIn` — search `"[Firm]" + "campus recruiting" + USC`.
2. **Not every row proves a dedicated USC-only pipeline** — most prove Marshall/TIS/TFA placement history or USC-hosted recruiting events.
3. **AUM figures are approximate** from public disclosures; verify before outreach.
