Research compiled from **official firm websites** and **non-USC university pages** (UCLA Anderson, CMU TartanConnect, etc.). USC sites (`marshall.usc.edu`, `careers.usc.edu`, etc.), RocketReach/SignalHire guesses, and LinkedIn-only profiles were excluded per scope.

**Excel deliverable:** [`Recruiters/`](Recruiters/) folder contains tiered `.xlsx` workbooks (Tier A verified, Tier A $1–10B focus, Tier B $1–10B, and master combined file).

## Methodology & Limitations

1. **Full matches are rare.** Most large buy-side firms publish generic campus inboxes (`blackrockcampusrecruiting@`, `undergradrecruiting@wellington.com`, etc.) without naming recruiters or listing USC alumni in TA roles on official pages.
2. **USC alumni density is high on the investing side** (PMs, analysts, partners) but those are not recruiters.
3. **USC Hub staff excluded:** Lauren Irwin, Cam Pham, `msbfinancecareerhub@marshall.usc.edu`, etc. — these are USC-side contacts, not firm recruiters.
4. **IB excluded:** Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, etc. were not searched for this list.
5. **"Every firm ever" is not achievable** from public firm/university pages alone; this reflects exhaustive search across major buy-side names, Fink Credit Pitch sponsors, and targeted `"University of Southern California" + talent/recruiting` queries on firm domains.
